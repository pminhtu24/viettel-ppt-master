## A

# Deck style: A) General Versatile — visual impact (executor-general.md)

- Vary the composition from page to page: image/text split (5:5 or 4:6), three equal cards, a hub with 4–6 nodes, a top band of key numbers over a chart, a Z-pattern of alternating blocks. Keep one composition per chapter, change it between chapters.
- Big icons (40–48px) or numbered circles lead each card or step; a large red key number or phrase is the focal point.
- Cover, chapter and ending pages use their red layouts boldly; content pages stay white with the rule-10 frame.
- Color: cards filled with their group color at fill-opacity 0.12, a solid icon in that color, the focal number red.
- Short, plain wording; one idea per card.

## B

# Deck style: B) General Consulting — data clarity (executor-consultant.md)

- KPI band: 3–4 cards in a row, each with an icon, a short metric name, the number 36–42px bold (red for the headline one) and one line of context from the source.
- Chart plus notes: the chart takes about two thirds of the width; the notes column on the right carries the source's own explanation, problems or next steps for those figures.
- A summary band under the title (`#F2F2F2`, red left stripe) when the source states one line that sums up the page.
- Direct value labels at the end of every bar, no legend when labels suffice; a target or plan line as a gray dashed line.
- Tables: bold header, zebra rows, numbers right-aligned, the status column colored.
- Color: white cards with a 6px stripe; color only on stripes, the focal number and status pills.

## C

# Deck style: C) Top Consulting — logical persuasion (executor-consultant-top.md)

- The title states the page's conclusion (only what its blocks support); the body proves it top-down: the key figure first, then the two or three supporting points.
- Every figure sits next to its reference: plan, target, previous period or benchmark, as the source gives it.
- Color is reserved: context in red at fill-opacity 0.4 or deep blue, solid red only on the one figure or bar that carries the conclusion.
- A strict grid with even gutters; no decoration that does not carry data.
